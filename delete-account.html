Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your PigPal account and all associated data at any time. Once deleted, your account and data cannot be recovered.

What will be deleted

  • Your account credentials and profile
  • All pig records, breeding logs, and farm data
  • Vaccination, feeding, and task history
  • Marketplace listings and transaction records
  • Cloud-synced backups associated with your account

To request deletion, send an email to us from the address linked to your PigPal account. We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation once complete.

✉ Request Account Deletion

This will open your email app with a pre-filled deletion request. Make sure to send it from the email address registered with your PigPal account.

For more information on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
