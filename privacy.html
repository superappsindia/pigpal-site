PigPal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

PigPal (the "App") is developed and provided by SuperApps ("we", "us", "our"). PigPal helps farmers manage piggery tasks such as breeding, feeding, vaccinations, weighing, inventory and transactions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we store it (including cloud storage), and how you can control it.

Scope

This policy applies to personal and farm-related data collected through the PigPal mobile and web applications, associated backend services (including Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Storage, cloud synchronization and our weight-estimation server), and any related support channels. It does not apply to information collected offline or by unaffiliated third parties.

Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Device & technical data: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and basic diagnostics for crash reports and troubleshooting.
  • App data you enter: farm records, animal tags, breeding logs, vaccination and deworming records, weights, feed and inventory data, financial transactions, and other farm management information you choose to store in the app.
  • Usage data: anonymous or pseudonymized analytics events (feature usage, timestamps) to help us improve the app.
  • Cloud sync data: when you enable cloud synchronization or backups, copies of your farm records are securely stored on cloud servers (for example, Google Firebase Firestore or Cloud Storage) under your account identifier.
  • Support data: messages, attachments, or screenshots you send to us when contacting support.
  • Marketplace listings: photos, descriptions, and contact details you choose to publish in PigPal Marketplace are visible to other users within the app and can be edited or deleted by you at any time.
  • Camera & location (Marketplace): when you create or edit a Marketplace listing, PigPal may request camera access to capture or attach listing photos and (optional) approximate location to tag your farm area. Location is never collected automatically and can be skipped; denying these permissions only limits those specific features.
  • Weight-by-camera photos: when you estimate a pig's weight from a photo, the photo is sent to our weight-estimation server to calculate the estimate. When you also enter a weight measured with a scale or weighing tape, an anonymised copy of the photo, the camera estimate and your measured weight are kept to improve the weight estimator (see "Machine learning and model improvement").
  • Anonymous farm data ("Help improve PigPal", your choice): after you first sign in, PigPal asks you once whether you want to help improve PigPal. If you say Yes, anonymous figures such as sale price, pig breed, litter size, piglet mortality and treatment records are shared with our servers to build regional benchmarks and improve PigPal's AI features. Your name, farm name, pig tag IDs, location and any personally identifiable information are never included. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may access the device's Google Advertising ID (AAID) and other anonymous identifiers to display and measure contextual ads.

How we use your data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality, both offline and through secure cloud synchronization.
  • Estimate pig weights from photos, and alert you to weight loss or slow growth based on your own weight records.
  • Send optional reminders or server-based notifications (if you enable them) for vaccinations, breeding, and feeding schedules.
  • Backup and restore your data safely across devices via encrypted cloud sync (if you opt in).
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues, including crash reporting and performance optimization.
  • Conduct analytics to improve features and user experience.
  • Respond to your support and feedback requests.
  • Build regional pig price benchmarks and farm comparisons from anonymised, collective farm data (when you say Yes to "Help improve PigPal").
  • Train and improve PigPal's machine learning features, such as the weight estimator (see below).
  • Support Marketplace features such as attaching photos and optionally tagging an approximate location on listings (only when you choose to use those features).

Farm & Market Insights — "Help improve PigPal"

Right after you first sign in, PigPal asks you one question: whether you want to help improve PigPal. Nothing is shared under this choice unless you tap Yes. If you say Yes, anonymous farm figures (such as sale price, pig breed, litter size, piglet mortality and treatment records) are shared with our servers. Your name, farm name, pig tag IDs, location, financial account details, and any personally identifiable information never leave your device as part of this feature. Contributions are grouped only by a one-way code that cannot be turned back into your account.

Saying Yes unlocks the Farm & Market Insights tab inside the app's Insights panel. This tab includes the Market Price Index (a regional average sale price computed from anonymous contributions) and, as it becomes available, regional benchmarks for breed performance, litter size, and piglet mortality. The same anonymous figures may also be used to train PigPal's AI features. If you say No, or later turn it off, the Farm & Market Insights tab is shown with a blurred overlay and the data within it is unavailable until you turn it back on.

The Business Insights tab (herd snapshot, feed balance, profit chart, break-even analysis, and health task alerts) is a separate paid feature and is not affected by this choice.

You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. Turning it off stops any further sharing of these figures immediately.

Machine learning and model improvement

Weight estimator (active). When you estimate a pig's weight from a photo and then enter a weight you measured with a scale or weighing tape, PigPal keeps an anonymised copy of that photo together with the camera estimate and your measured weight. We use these pairs to train and check the weight estimator, which makes photo weights more accurate for you and for other farmers. This is part of the weight-by-camera feature and applies whether or not you chose "Help improve PigPal".

The copy contains no name, farm name, phone number, pig tag number or account ID. Location and other hidden photo details (EXIF data such as GPS position, device model and time taken) are removed on your phone before upload. The copies are readable only by PigPal's model-training systems, never by other users. If you do not want to contribute, simply do not use weight-by-camera; manual weight entry works as before. You can ask us to delete photos you contributed using the contact address below.

Other AI features. If you said Yes to "Help improve PigPal", the anonymous farm figures described above may also be used to train other predictive features (for example, feed cost forecasting or health pattern detection). Raw farm records, animal tags, financial figures, and personal identifiers are never used in identifiable form for model training. Turning the choice off in Settings → Privacy choices stops this and does not affect any core functionality.

Cloud storage and synchronization

PigPal allows you to optionally sync your farm data to our secure cloud infrastructure (powered by Google Firebase). When enabled, your records are encrypted in transit and stored in a secure cloud database under your authenticated user ID.

Cloud sync enables data backup, restoration on multiple devices, and server-based notifications (such as reminders before due tasks). You can disable sync at any time from within the app's settings. Disabling sync will stop further uploads but will not automatically delete existing cloud copies. You may request full deletion of your cloud data via email.

Important: All cloud data is stored in Firebase data centers operated by Google Cloud, which may be located in different regions depending on your country of use. Data transfers comply with applicable data protection regulations.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: trusted third parties (e.g., Google Firebase, Crashlytics, or analytics partners) who help us operate the app, store data, or send notifications. They process data under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • "Help improve PigPal" (your choice): if you say Yes, anonymous farm figures are shared with our servers to compute regional benchmarks and improve PigPal's AI features. No personally identifiable or farm-identifiable data is included.
  • Machine learning infrastructure: anonymised weight-by-camera photos and weights, and anonymous farm figures you chose to share, are processed on PigPal's own servers and Google Firebase for model training. No personally identifiable data is shared.
  • Advertising partners: Google AdMob may process the device Advertising ID (AAID) and related signals to display and measure contextual ads. No personal or farm data is shared for ad targeting.
  • Legal requests: when required by law or to protect our rights, property, or users' safety.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to data protection safeguards.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud-stored data (if enabled) is retained as long as your account is active or until you request deletion. Anonymised training data (weight-by-camera photos and anonymous farm figures) is kept for as long as it is useful for improving PigPal's models, unless you ask us to delete your contributions. Support and diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting or as required by law.

You may request deletion of your cloud data, contributed training photos and associated account by contacting us at hello@superappsdomain.com. Deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable timeframe and confirmed once complete.

Security

We apply industry-standard security practices, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS), secure authentication (Firebase Auth), and limited data access on a need-to-know basis. However, no electronic storage is 100% secure — we advise users to avoid storing sensitive personal information (like financial credentials) in the app.

Analytics, crash reporting & opt-out

PigPal uses analytics and crash reporting (for example, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) to improve stability and performance. These tools collect anonymous or pseudonymized data. You may opt out of analytics collection through the in-app privacy settings without affecting core features.

Permissions & controls

You can control camera, location, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings. Denying a permission will only limit the related feature (for example, weighing pigs by photo, adding listing photos or tagging approximate location); the rest of PigPal will continue to work.

Children

PigPal is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If such information is discovered, it will be deleted promptly upon notification.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal or farm-related data stored by PigPal.
  • Request correction, export, or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics, cloud synchronization, or "Help improve PigPal" at any time — the last from Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Ask us to delete weight-by-camera photos you contributed.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at the address below. Verification may be required before action.
